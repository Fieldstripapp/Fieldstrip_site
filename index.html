A record of your safe
that never leaves your safe.

No cloud. No account. Nobody watching you clean your guns. Fieldstrip keeps your whole bench on your device — step-by-step guides, ammunition and magazine tracking, and a complete logbook for every firearm you own. It’s a workbench companion: the phone in your pocket at the range, the tablet propped on the mat at the bench.

1.0

The product

Guided cleaning, the way a manual should work. Fieldstrip walks you through field-stripping and cleaning your specific firearm step by step — every guide gated behind a safety check confirming the firearm is clear. Where a deeper clean is documented, the guide goes there. Where the work belongs to a gunsmith, or shouldn’t be done at all, the app says so. Fieldstrip never invents steps.

GUIDE

Guided clean player

Step-by-step field-strip and cleaning sequences, per model, with a safety gate on every guide.

TRACK

Ammo locker

Ammunition inventory with barcode check-in, automatic round-count deduction as you log range sessions, and low-stock flags.

TRACK

Magazines & rotation

Magazines as first-class items — capacity, role, and carry-rotation timers.

MAINTAIN

Care reminders

Cleaning intervals, spring-life tracking, and scheduled reminders delivered as phone notifications. Factory maintenance notes surfaced where manufacturers publish them.

RECORD

Service, mods & zero

A complete logbook per firearm: service and modification records, zero records by load and distance, and suppressor fouling logs.

Safety first, always. Fieldstrip is a maintenance aid, not a substitute for your firearm's owner's manual or for professional gunsmithing. Every guide begins with a verified-clear safety gate, and the app is intended for lawful owners aged 18 and over.

AT THE BENCHBuilt for the cleaning session

The serious hobbyist doesn't strip a firearm on a phone screen. Fieldstrip runs full-screen on a tablet propped at the bench — large type, step-by-step pacing you set by hand, and records that update as you work. Everything runs fully offline, on phone and tablet alike, so the cleaning mat is the only thing on the bench that needs your attention.

Privacy posture

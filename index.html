The product

Guided cleaning, the way a manual should work. Fieldstrip walks you through field-stripping and cleaning your specific firearm with animated, step-by-step diagrams rendered from 3D models — each part highlighted as you work, each step gated behind a safety check confirming the firearm is clear. Procedures follow manufacturer guidance, including optional deep-clean paths where the factory manual documents them, and clear stop-points where work belongs to a qualified gunsmith.

GUIDE

Guided clean player

Illustrated field-strip and cleaning sequences with animated part diagrams, safety gates, and per-model procedures for pistols, shotguns, and rifles.

DIAGRAM

Parts diagrams

Exploded IPB-style views with numbered callouts, built from the same 3D renders as the guides. Tap a part to identify it.

TRACK

Ammo locker

Ammunition inventory with barcode check-in, automatic round-count deduction as you log range sessions, and low-stock flags.

TRACK

Magazines & rotation

Magazines as first-class items — capacity, role, and carry-rotation timers.

MAINTAIN

Care reminders

Cleaning intervals, spring-life tracking, and scheduled reminders delivered as phone notifications. Factory maintenance notes surfaced where manufacturers publish them.

RECORD

Service, mods & zero

A complete logbook per firearm: service and modification records, zero records by load and distance, and suppressor fouling logs.

Safety first, always. Fieldstrip is a maintenance aid, not a substitute for your firearm's owner's manual or for professional gunsmithing. Every guide begins with a verified-clear safety gate, and the app is intended for lawful owners aged 18 and over.

AT THE BENCHBuilt for the cleaning session

The serious hobbyist doesn't strip a firearm on a phone screen. Fieldstrip runs full-screen on a tablet propped at the bench — large-format part diagrams, step-by-step pacing you set by hand, and records that update as you work. Everything runs fully offline, on phone and tablet alike, so the cleaning mat is the only thing on the bench that needs your attention.

Capability index

Catalog
457 firearms & suppressors indexed — 175 pistols, 134 rifles, 50 shotguns, 49 revolvers, 49 suppressors.
Guided platforms
12 platform families routed to 11 illustrated guides — Glock, 1911, AR-15/10, X95, 870 & 500 pump, double- and single-action revolver, and serviceable / sealed / rimfire suppressors.
Models with a guide
174 of 457 models field-strip with a guide — every revolver and every suppressor, plus the Glock, 1911, AR, X95, 870 and 500 families.
Guide depth
14 steps in the deepest guide — the Glock, field strip through deep clean, each step a rendered and marked-up part diagram.
Deep-clean branches
5 guides continue past the field strip into a full deep clean — Glock, 1911, both revolver actions, and the 500 pump.
Detail-inset renders

Trackers

Architecture
0 network permissions in the release build — no INTERNET access is even declared, so everything you log stays on the device. See privacy posture ↓

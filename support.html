QIs the app available yet?

Fieldstrip is in active development. The Android release comes first, with iOS following. Email us if you'd like to be notified at launch.

QWhere is my data stored?

On your device. Fieldstrip is local-first — no account, no cloud sync, no ads. Deleting the app deletes your data. Details in the privacy policy.

QWhich firearms are supported?

Guided cleaning launches with popular pistol, pump-action shotgun, and modern sporting rifle platforms, and the catalog grows continuously. Each guide is built per-model from 3D-rendered diagrams and checked against the manufacturer's published procedures.

QDoes Fieldstrip replace my owner's manual?

No. Fieldstrip is a maintenance aid. Your manufacturer's manual is the authority for your specific firearm, and anything beyond routine field-strip cleaning belongs with a qualified gunsmith. The app is intended for lawful owners aged 18 and over.

QFound a bug? Have a model request?

Email contact@fieldstripllc.com with your device model and what you saw — screenshots welcome. Model requests are logged and prioritized by demand.

Support

Getting started, answers to common questions, and how to reach a human. Fieldstrip is a maintenance aid — always follow your firearm's owner's manual, and treat every firearm as loaded until you have verified otherwise.

A · GETTING STARTED

Your first clean

  1. Add a firearm

    Open My Safe and search the catalog — pistols, revolvers, shotguns, rifles, and suppressors. Tap your make and model to add it. The free tier holds two firearms; Pro removes the limit.

  2. Clear it first — the safety gate

    Every guide opens on a safety gate. Confirm the firearm is unloaded and pointed in a safe direction by checking each box; the guide will not begin the teardown until all checks are made. This step is not optional and cannot be skipped.

  3. Follow the guide

    Step through the field-strip one step at a time with Next Step. Every step has a photo slot — attach your own pictures of your own gun as you go, and the guide becomes yours. Deep-clean paths, care reminders, and record-keeping are there when you want them.

B · FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions

QIs my data private?

Yes. Fieldstrip is local-first: your firearms, ammunition, magazines, maintenance history, and reminders are stored on your device — no account, no cloud sync, no ads, and nothing sold or shared. Full details are in the privacy policy.

QDoes the app need an internet connection?

No. Guides and records work without a signal. No account, nothing in the background, and nothing you record leaves your phone.

QHow do I request a firearm that isn't in the catalog?

Email contact@fieldstripllc.com with the make, model, and variant — the most-requested platforms get built first. In the app, firearm requests are a Pro feature; from here, a plain email is all it takes.

QHow do I back up my data?

Use the app's own Backup → Save to Device. It writes a backup file to your Downloads folder — keep a copy somewhere safe, and restore it on a new phone from the same screen. Keep that backup current — deleting the app without a backup deletes the data, and we hold no copy on any server.

QDoes Fieldstrip replace my owner's manual?

No. Fieldstrip is a maintenance aid. Your manufacturer's manual is the authority for your specific firearm, and anything beyond routine field-strip cleaning belongs with a qualified gunsmith. The app is intended for lawful owners aged 18 and over.

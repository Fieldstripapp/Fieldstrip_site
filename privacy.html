Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE: SEPTEMBER 2, 2026 · APPLIES TO THE FIELDSTRIP APP AND FIELDSTRIPAPP.COM

1The short version

Your records never leave your phone. Fieldstrip is local-first. The records you keep in the app — firearms, ammunition, magazines, maintenance history, zero data, reminders — are stored on your device. We do not operate accounts, we do not run ads, and we do not sell, rent, or share personal data. No account, no identifier, no telemetry. Everything you log stays on the device. The app goes online only when you ask it to — to check for a guide while you search or when you tap Check for guide, to download a guide you’ve said yes to, or to send a request you typed. When it does, the server sees an IP asking for a particular guide at a particular time — and none of your records. No account, no tracking, and nothing you record is sent.

2Data the app stores

All app data is stored locally on your device using the operating system's application storage. This includes firearm records you create, ammunition inventory, magazine details, maintenance and service logs, zero records, and reminder schedules. This data never leaves your device unless you export or back it up yourself using your device's own tools. When the app goes online (a guide check while you search or when you tap Check for guide, a guide download, or a request you typed), it sends none of these records.

3Data we collect

Fieldstrip L.L.C. does not collect personal data through the app. We do not require registration, we do not collect names, email addresses, locations, or contact lists, and we do not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for care reminders you set. They are not sent from our servers.
  • Camera access, if you grant it, is used to scan ammunition barcodes on-device and to take the photos you attach to your own records. Photos you attach are stored on your device only and never transmitted.
  • Crash reports, if provided by your device's app store, reach us in aggregate, anonymized form through Google Play developer tools, governed by Google's own policies.

4This website

fieldstripapp.com is a static informational site. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log requests per Google's privacy policy. If you email us, we retain the correspondence to respond and for our records.

5Children

Fieldstrip is intended for lawful firearm owners aged 18 and over. The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

6Your choices

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes the data. To remove correspondence you have sent us, email contact@fieldstripllc.com and we will delete it.

7Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes affecting the app will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE: NOVEMBER 22, 2026 · APPLIES TO THE FIELDSTRIP APP AND FIELDSTRIPAPP.COM

1The short version

Your records never leave your device. Fieldstrip is local-first. The records you keep in the app — firearms, suppressors, ammunition, magazines, maintenance history, zero data, reminders, photos and notes — are stored on your device. We do not operate accounts, we do not run ads, and we do not sell, rent, or share personal data. No account, no identifier, no telemetry. Records leave the device only when you make a backup or an export, or sync to your own second device over your Wi-Fi. The app goes online only on your tap: to check for new guns, to download a guide, or to send a request you typed. Nothing from your safe is sent.

2Data the app stores

What stays on the device. Everything you log stays on this device: your firearms, suppressors, ammo, photos and notes. There are no accounts and no email. There are no analytics and no ads. The app does not read your location or your contacts.

All app data is stored locally on your device using the operating system's application storage. This includes firearm records you create, ammunition inventory, magazine details, maintenance and service logs, zero records, and reminder schedules.

3When records leave the device

Only when you do it. You save a backup or an export file, or you sync to your own second device. Sync goes straight from one device to the other over your Wi-Fi. There is no server. While a pairing code is showing, the device announces “Fieldstrip” and its model on that Wi-Fi so your other device can find it. A backup or export file goes where you choose to save it.

4When the app goes online

Only on your tap, for three things: to check for new guns, to download a guide, and to send a request you typed. Checks and guides come from library.fieldstripapp.com, which is hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, it sees an IP address asking for a file at a time. Nothing from your safe is sent.

5Data we collect

Fieldstrip L.L.C. collects one thing through the app: a request you choose to send. We do not require registration, we do not collect names, email addresses, locations, or contact lists, and we do not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

A request you send. A request carries what you typed and nothing else: the name of the gun, or the bug or idea as you typed it. It goes to fieldstrip-receiver.fieldstrip.workers.dev, which runs on Cloudflare. Fieldstrip keeps it to act on it. It carries no id.

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for care reminders you set. They are not sent from our servers.
  • Camera access, if you grant it, is used to scan ammunition barcodes on-device and to take the photos you attach to your own records. Photos you attach stay on your device with the rest of your records.
  • Crash reports: the app has no crash reporting of its own. Crash reports, if provided by your device's app store, reach us through the Google Play and App Store developer tools, governed by Google's and Apple's own policies.

6Buying Pro

Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad) handles the purchase. The app asks the store on this device whether Pro is owned, including when the app starts. It sends nothing from your safe. The payment details and the store account belong to Google Play or the App Store, and the app never sees them.

7This website

fieldstripapp.com is a static informational site. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log requests per Google's privacy policy. If you email us, we retain the correspondence to respond and for our records.

8Children

Fieldstrip is intended for lawful firearm owners aged 18 and over. The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9Your choices

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes the data on that device. Backups, export files, and the copy on your own second device stay where they are until you delete them. A request sent from the app carries no id, so Fieldstrip cannot pick out which requests are yours. To remove correspondence you have sent us, email contact@fieldstripllc.com and we will delete it.

10Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
